# Code Of Conduct

This project expects respectful, direct, and constructive collaboration.

## Expected Behavior

- Be professional and specific.
- Focus on technical issues, not personal attacks.
- Assume good intent, but be clear when behavior is harmful or disruptive.
- Accept feedback and course-corrections without hostility.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- harassment or intimidation
- discriminatory language or targeting
- repeated bad-faith disruption
- doxxing, threats, or abusive conduct

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, lock, or close content that violates these expectations and may limit participation when necessary to keep the project usable.
